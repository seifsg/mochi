A cute desk buddy that pops helpful tips on your screen.

Mochi sits on top of your windows and, now and then, says one short tip that fits the app you are in, the time of day, or the moment you come back from a break. No AI, no account, no internet.

Small tips, right when they fit

  • 🧭

    Knows your app

    Shortcuts for VS Code, Excel, Word, your browser, Teams, Slack, File Explorer and more.

  • ☕

    Notices your breaks

    Away for five minutes? Mochi dozes off and welcomes you back with a gentle nudge.

  • 🌙

    Fits your day

    Morning focus, afternoon stretches, evening wrap-ups and late-night reminders to rest.

  • 🫧

    Stays out of the way

    Clicks pass through empty space, the bubble hides on a tap, and Mute is one click in the tray.

Install

  1. Download the installer with the button above (Windows 10 or 11).
  2. Run it. Mochi installs for your user only and appears at the bottom-right of your screen.
  3. Drag Mochi anywhere. Right-click the tray icon to show a tip, mute, start with Windows or quit.

Prefer to build it yourself? The README has the three commands.

Mochi is free and open source (MIT). It never goes online: tips live in a local list.
